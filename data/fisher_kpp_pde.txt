FISHER-KOLMOGOROV-PETROVSKY-PISKUNOV (FKPP) REACTION-DIFFUSION MODEL

We study the spread of a beneficial allele u(x, t) through a population
living along a one-dimensional habitat of length L = 40.

The model is the non-dimensional Fisher-KPP equation:

    du/dt = D * d2u/dx2 + r * u * (1 - u)

where

- u(x, t) is the allele frequency (0 < u < 1),
- D is the diffusion coefficient describing random dispersal,
- r is the intrinsic growth rate of the allele.

Parameter values reported in the paper:

    D = 0.2
    r = 1.0

Initial condition (a small localised inoculum in the middle of the habitat):

    u(x, 0) = exp( -((x - 20)^2) / 4 )

Boundary conditions: zero-flux (Neumann) at both ends of the domain.

Domain:

    x in [0, 40],  t in [0, 20]

The paper shows that the solution develops a travelling wave front moving
outward from the centre with asymptotic speed c = 2 * sqrt(D * r), which for
these parameters equals 2 * sqrt(0.2) ~= 0.894.

Task for the modelling agent: reproduce this reaction-diffusion PDE, simulate
the spreading wave, and report whether the observed front speed is close to
the theoretically predicted speed.
